Analytic for the Transport Management System (TMS):

- Vehicles have an analytic distribution. Setting the tractor or the trailer
  in the task form adds it to the analytic distribution of the task sale order
  line (and therefore of its invoice lines), replacing the one of the previous
  vehicles and keeping the rest of the distribution. Vehicles assigned from the
  planning kanban or elsewhere don't change the distribution.
- Vehicles can be linked to the analytic account that represents them.
- The tractor, trailer, driver and division are available in the invoices
  analysis.
