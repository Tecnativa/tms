Analytic for the Transport Management System (TMS):

- Vehicles have an analytic distribution, added to the analytic distribution
  of the sale order lines (and therefore of their tasks and invoice lines)
  where the vehicle is set as tractor or trailer. Changing the tractor or the
  trailer replaces the distribution of the previous vehicle by the new one,
  keeping the rest of the line distribution.
- Vehicles can be linked to the analytic account that represents them.
- The tractor, trailer, driver and division are available in the invoices
  analysis.
