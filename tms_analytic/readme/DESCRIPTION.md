Analytic for the Transport Management System (TMS):

- Vehicles have an analytic distribution. Setting the tractor or the trailer
  on a sale order line or its task (form, planning kanban, timeline...) adds it
  to the analytic distribution of the sale order line (and therefore of its
  invoice lines), replacing the one of the previous vehicles and keeping the
  rest of the distribution.
- Vehicles can be linked to the analytic account that represents them.
- The tractor, trailer, driver and division are available in the invoices
  analysis.
